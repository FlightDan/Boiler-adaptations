# X2 Mini Arc G3 Extreme PL1 range

The X2 Mini adaptation derives three control files from the locked public CPU
baseline. The provider reports an 8 W PL1 minimum only for the X2 Mini DMI and
Intel Arc G3 Extreme CPU. Other identities keep the 10 W minimum. PL2 retains
its existing limits. The native SteamOS TdpLimit1 and Boiler Decky slider both
consume the provider's PL1 range.

On 2026-11-08, on SteamOS kernel 7.2.4-valve1-1-neptune-72-g5ab4af5e2eb9,
both package RAPL interfaces read back 8 W. After deploying the derived files,
the game-session SteamOS Manager reported a minimum of 8 W and its property
setter applied 8 W on both interfaces. Boiler's power helper also reported an
8 W minimum and accepted an 8 W request. Both interfaces were restored to
16 W after testing. Decky Loader was active after reload. A visual check of
both sliders and testing under gameplay load remain open.

The derived files were applied to the tested device with source verification
and backups of the originals. Installer, retained-maintenance upgrade and
package recovery integration remain open. This record is source and
device-test evidence, not a new package or release.
